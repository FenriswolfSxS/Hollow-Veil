HOLLOW VEIL — AMBIENT EMBER FIELD

Added a lightweight global ember layer that appears on the landing page and every interior page.

Behavior:
- 34 deterministic, tiny ember particles rise slowly from below the viewport.
- Particles vary subtly in size, brightness, drift, blur, speed, and starting position.
- The field is pointer-events:none and cannot block buttons, links, navigation, or roster controls.
- Particle rendering uses CSS transforms and opacity for low overhead.
- Mobile hides half of the particles to reduce visual and GPU load.
- prefers-reduced-motion disables the effect entirely.
- Existing theme, roots transition, roster, navigation, and landing page were not changed.
