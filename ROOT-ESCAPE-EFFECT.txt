Hollow Veil — Root Escape Effect

Clicking the Hollow Veil crest in the navigation now:
1. Locks the navigation to prevent repeat clicks.
2. Animates dark tree roots across the screen from both sides.
3. Reveals “YOU TRIED TO LEAVE.”
4. Flashes the screen and returns to the landing page.
5. Shows “THERE IS NO ESCAPE” over the landing artwork, then fades it away.

The existing site theme, landing artwork, roster, job icons, and navigation styling were not changed.
The effect includes mobile adjustments and a reduced-motion accessibility fallback.
